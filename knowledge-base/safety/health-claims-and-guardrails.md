# Health Claims, Trust and Safety Guardrails

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Renomedy is not a source of medical advice, diagnosis or treatment recommendations and is not intended to replace healthcare professionals. This boundary is stated on the current public website.
2. Do not say Renomedy diagnoses, prescribes, recommends dose changes or makes independent clinical decisions.
3. Do not claim prescription interpretation is accurate unless verified evidence and approved wording exist.
4. Do not promise that the product prevents all missed doses, medication errors, hospitalizations or adverse outcomes.
5. Do not present an unrecorded event as proof a medicine was not taken.
6. Do not present a supply estimate as a guarantee.
7. Do not make unverified claims about compliance, certification, clinical validation, encryption, security audits or privacy guarantees.
8. Do not fabricate testimonials, user counts, clinical statistics, research citations or outcomes.
9. Do not request identifiable prescription images or private health details in public marketing channels.
10. Route material health claims and ambiguous product claims to human review.

## Current-stage claims

Because the public site says the product is in development and not yet available:
- Do not say users can download, install, subscribe to or start using Renomedy now.
- Do not portray concept screens as live UI.
- Do not promise feature availability or a launch date.
- Prefer “exploring,” “in development,” “concept,” and “designed around” where accurate.

## Safer phrasing patterns

Prefer:
- “Renomedy is exploring ways to help families organize everyday medicine-routine details.”
- “A concept for keeping routine details and family context together.”
- “Designed around family coordination, alongside guidance from healthcare professionals.”

Avoid:
- “Reads every prescription perfectly.”
- “Ensures your parent never misses a dose.”
- “Guarantees medicine is always available.”
- “Keeps your family completely safe.”
- “Clinically proven” without documented evidence and explicit approval.

## QA outcome definitions

- **Pass:** Claim is supported by the approved source ledger and accurately reflects concept status.
- **Warning:** Claim depends on a feature, source or wording that needs owner confirmation.
- **Block:** Claim presents the product as available, invents evidence, promises a medical outcome or materially misrepresents functionality.

Automated checks are only a screening layer; human review remains required.
