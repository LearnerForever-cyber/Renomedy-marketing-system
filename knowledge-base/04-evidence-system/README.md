# Block 4 — Evidence System

**Purpose:** Keep claims traceable, decisions explicit, and learning cumulative so agents cannot silently turn assumptions into facts.

## Stages
- [Stage 10 — Sources & Fact Ledger](stage-10-sources-fact-ledger/README.md) — working draft; owner approval pending.
- [Stage 11 — Decisions & Open Questions](stage-11-decisions-open-questions/README.md) — working draft; owner approval pending.
- [Stage 12 — Experiments & Continuous Learning](stage-12-experiments-continuous-learning/README.md) — working draft; owner approval pending.

## Evidence standard
Every material statement should be traceable to a source, have a status and date, and disclose what it does not prove. Existing source-register.md and fact-ledger.md remain authoritative reference files; stage documents must not silently contradict or replace them.

## Numbering
Each stage has its own folder. Files are prefixed 10.x, 11.x, and 12.x respectively.
