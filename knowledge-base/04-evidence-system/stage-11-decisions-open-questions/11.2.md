# 11.2 — Open-Question Backlog and Prioritization

**Status:** Proposed backlog standard — owner approval pending

## Question fields
Question ID; exact question; why it matters; current answer/status; evidence already available; evidence needed; impact if wrong; urgency; dependencies; accountable person; next action; due/review date; affected documents; closure evidence.

## Priority rubric
- **P0 — Safety/blocker:** unresolved question could cause a misleading health, privacy, security, availability, or legal claim, or block a responsible next step.
- **P1 — Critical strategy:** answer changes target audience, initial scope, or current CTA.
- **P2 — Important design/operations:** answer affects consistency or execution but does not create immediate high-risk claims.
- **P3 — Later refinement:** useful but not necessary for the next safe decision.

Priority is a proposed framework; owner should confirm it fits the team's workflow.

## Closure rule
Close a question only when the answer is supported by appropriate evidence and the relevant source or decision record is updated. If evidence is unavailable, keep it open and record the reason. Do not convert “not answered” into “no issue.”

## Current high-priority questions from existing backlog
- Is the public website still canonical and what is the current product status?
- Which capabilities are actually Live, Prototype, Planned, Dropped, or Unknown?
- What research supports the initial audience and pain points?
- What are the approved brand assets and design tokens?
- Which privacy/security measures and specialist reviews are evidenced?
- What is the current CTA and does its destination work?

These are copied as themes from the existing backlog, not newly resolved facts.
