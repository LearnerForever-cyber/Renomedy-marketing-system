# 11.1 — Decision Register and Approval States

**Status:** Proposed decision record — owner approval pending

## Decision record fields
ID; title; date raised; accountable human; decision needed; context; options; evidence; risks/trade-offs; decision; rationale; status; approval scope; review trigger; related source and stage documents.

## Decision statuses
- **Proposed:** suggested, not decided.
- **Under review:** evidence or stakeholder input is being collected.
- **Approved:** the accountable owner explicitly approved the decision for a defined scope; record date and scope.
- **Rejected:** considered and not selected; retain rationale.
- **Superseded:** replaced by a newer decision, with a link to it.
- **Blocked:** cannot responsibly proceed until a prerequisite or evidence gap is resolved.

## Approval rules
- Publication to GitHub is not approval.
- Repetition in chat is not approval unless the owner explicitly confirms the decision and scope.
- An agent can draft options and identify trade-offs but cannot silently make consequential company, clinical, privacy, or legal decisions.
- Owner approval does not substitute for professional review when the decision requires it.
- Preserve dissent and evidence limitations in the record.

## Decision hygiene
Use reversible, low-risk tests when uncertainty is high. Do not reopen settled choices without new evidence, changed constraints, material inconsistency, or an explicit owner request. Do not leave a consequential decision hidden in chat; transfer it to the decision record.
