# 11.3 — Governance, Ownership and Review Cadence

**Status:** Proposed governance — owner approval pending

## Roles that must be assigned
- Product-truth owner: confirms current capability and availability evidence.
- Brand owner: approves canonical identity, visual tokens, and message hierarchy.
- Research owner: approves protocol, consent, and interpretation of findings.
- Safety reviewer: reviews health-sensitive or potentially harmful claims.
- Privacy/security reviewer: verifies data-practice and security claims where relevant.
- Marketing approver: signs off on final public content and CTA.
- Knowledge-base steward: maintains sources, statuses, links, versions, and consistency.

One person may cover multiple roles, but the accountable person for each decision must be explicit. The reviewed repository does not assign named owners for these roles.

## Review cadence
- Before each campaign: recheck availability, feature claims, CTA, and safety.
- When a product capability changes: update product truth, fact ledger, source register, and affected copy guidance.
- After each research session or experiment: record findings, counterexamples, and decisions.
- At a regular owner-selected interval: review open questions, stale sources, superseded decisions, and stage status.

The exact recurring interval is not established; do not invent a weekly/monthly commitment.

## Escalation
Pause publication when material evidence conflicts, a high-risk claim lacks support, consent/data handling is unclear, or the CTA leads to an unverified destination. Record the blocker and the evidence needed to resolve it.

## Exit checklist
- [ ] Named owners and backup reviewers recorded.
- [ ] Approval states used consistently.
- [ ] Review cadence selected by owner.
- [ ] Open questions have owners and next actions.
