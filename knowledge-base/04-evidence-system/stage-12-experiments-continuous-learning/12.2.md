# 12.2 — Experiment Log and Learning Records

**Status:** Proposed log format — owner approval pending

## Required log fields
Experiment ID; link to approved brief; start/end dates; owner; version of stimulus; actual method; sample/recruitment details; deviations from plan; raw evidence location; observed results; missing data; negative findings; analysis; limitations; decision; follow-up; approver; related fact-ledger and stage updates.

## Result labels
- **Supports hypothesis in this sample:** observed evidence is consistent with the hypothesis but does not establish universal truth.
- **Contradicts hypothesis:** observed evidence conflicts with the expected result.
- **Mixed:** some outcomes support and others contradict.
- **Inconclusive:** data quality, sample, or execution does not support a useful conclusion.
- **Not run:** planned but no data collected.

Avoid the unqualified label “proven.” Even a positive experiment has a defined context and limitations.

## Analysis discipline
Separate raw observation from interpretation and decision. Record any deviations from the predeclared plan. Include failures and null results. Do not retroactively change the hypothesis or success threshold without documenting the change and treating the analysis accordingly.
