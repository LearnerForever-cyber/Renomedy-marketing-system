# 10.2 — Fact Ledger Schema and Claim Rules

**Status:** Proposed governance standard — owner approval pending

## Required claim fields
For each material claim, record:
1. Claim ID and precise wording.
2. Evidence status: Publicly stated, Owner-context, Verified behavior, Hypothesis, Unconfirmed, Approved, or Superseded.
3. Source IDs and exact location.
4. Date checked and evidence owner/reviewer.
5. What is supported and what is not supported.
6. Allowed use, caveats, and prohibited interpretations.
7. Owner approval status and scope.
8. Recheck trigger/date.

## Existing fact ledger rules
The current ledger says that claims not marked Publicly stated or Approved must be avoided, labelled as concept/hypothesis, or sent for human confirmation. It specifically marks AI-assisted prescription understanding, dose-event tracking, and refill/supply-gap estimation as owner-context rather than live features; production stack, clinical validation/certification, specific security implementation, product-market fit, and validated retention are unconfirmed.

## Status definitions
- **Publicly stated:** the dated source explicitly says it.
- **Owner-context:** internal project material describes it; not independently verified.
- **Verified behavior:** directly checked in a relevant environment, with scope and date recorded.
- **Hypothesis:** requires testing.
- **Unconfirmed:** evidence is insufficient or missing.
- **Approved:** an accountable human explicitly approves the exact claim for a defined use; approval does not replace specialist verification.
- **Superseded:** replaced by newer evidence or a decision; preserve history.

## No status laundering
A claim does not become verified because it appears in multiple AI-generated documents, because the owner once mentioned it, or because a source is linked. The evidence must support the exact statement. A source can support existence of a design but not working behaviour; support working behaviour but not general availability; or support availability but not clinical effectiveness.

## Update rule
When evidence changes, update the fact ledger and any affected stage documents in the same review cycle. Record the old status, new status, reason, evidence, date, and reviewer. Never erase a meaningful uncertainty just to simplify a report.
