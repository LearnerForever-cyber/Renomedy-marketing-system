# 10.3 — Source Review, Conflict and Freshness Protocol

**Status:** Proposed protocol — owner approval pending

## Freshness rules
- Recheck public availability, launch status, pricing, CTAs, and current feature claims close to publication.
- Recheck official policies and regulations from authoritative current sources when the task depends on them.
- Recheck brand files against the current canonical design source before changing tokens.
- Date research findings and explain sample/context limits; do not treat old user feedback as current without reason.
- A retrieval failure is not evidence that a source is offline or that its content changed.

## Conflict handling
1. State the conflicting claims and sources.
2. Compare source dates, authority, and scope.
3. Determine whether both statements could be true in different contexts or times.
4. If not resolved, mark Conflicting and pause affected public claims.
5. Ask the accountable human or relevant specialist to decide.
6. Record the decision and preserve the earlier evidence.

## Review log format
Date; reviewer; files/sources checked; claims affected; changed statuses; unresolved conflicts; approval requested; next action; next review date.
