# 7.3 — Evidence Evaluation and Validation Decisions

**Status:** Proposed evaluation standard — owner approval pending

## Evidence strength
Use the following as a practical hierarchy, not a universal scientific grading system:
1. Repeated observation of the target task or existing workaround in context.
2. Specific recent examples corroborated across independent participants.
3. Behavioural follow-through, such as agreeing to a clearly explained next research step.
4. Unprompted descriptions of pain or existing workarounds.
5. Reactions to a concept after neutral explanation.
6. General compliments, hypothetical intent, or answers to leading questions.

Record the method and limitations; do not imply statistical representativeness from a small qualitative sample.

## Synthesis rules
- Cluster observations without erasing differences between participants.
- Keep counterexamples and negative findings visible.
- Separate the frequency of a theme in the sample from prevalence in the market.
- Do not turn correlation or participant belief into a causal claim.
- Note recruitment and interviewer bias.
- Record uncertainty and alternative explanations.

## Decision outcomes
- **Continue testing:** evidence is mixed or the decision remains high-impact.
- **Refine:** a recurring friction or misunderstanding suggests a change to test.
- **Narrow segment:** one group demonstrates stronger observed need in the sampled evidence, subject to bias and further testing.
- **Pause or reject:** the hypothesis is contradicted, unsafe, or unsupported at the required threshold.
- **Insufficient evidence:** sample or method cannot support a decision.

## Before calling a hypothesis validated
Define the evidence threshold in advance, document results and counterexamples, explain sample limitations, link the source material, and record the human decision-maker. Do not invent numeric thresholds after seeing the results just to declare success.

## Current state
The reviewed repository provides no completed interview dataset or validated customer findings. All hypotheses remain unvalidated until evidence is collected and recorded.
