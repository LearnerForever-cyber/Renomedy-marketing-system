# 9.3 — Reporting, Attribution and Data Quality

**Status:** Proposed reporting standard — owner approval pending

## Reporting minimum
Every report must include period, objective, audience/channel, metric definitions, source, sample size or denominator, result, comparison basis, limitations, anomalies, and recommended next decision. Identify which observations are measured, estimated, or qualitative.

## Attribution
Do not assign credit to a channel without a defined attribution method. Record UTM or source tagging conventions only if actually implemented. Direct traffic, cross-device journeys, private sharing, and platform reporting limitations can make attribution incomplete; explain what cannot be known.

## Data quality checks
- Are events defined consistently?
- Are duplicate, bot, test, and internal events handled transparently?
- Are timestamps and time zones consistent?
- Are missing values visible?
- Did the tracking setup change during the reporting period?
- Does the denominator match the question?
- Is personal or sensitive information being collected unnecessarily?

## Privacy and access
Collect only data needed for the stated decision, follow applicable requirements and approved notices, restrict access appropriately, and set retention rules through an owner-approved process. Do not claim a particular privacy or security control is implemented without evidence.

## Current reporting status
The reviewed sources do not confirm analytics tooling, event instrumentation, attribution setup, or baseline metrics. Treat all such implementation details as unknown until checked.
