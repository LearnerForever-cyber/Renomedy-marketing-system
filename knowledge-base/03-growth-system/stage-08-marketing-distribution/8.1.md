# 8.1 — Channel Strategy and Readiness

**Status:** Proposed framework — owner approval pending

## Current facts and unknowns
The reviewed sources do not establish which marketing channels are currently active, which have produced results, the available budget, or which audience can be reached efficiently. Do not describe any channel as proven or active without campaign records.

## Channel evaluation framework
Evaluate candidate channels by:
- fit with the intended audience and their information habits;
- ability to explain a concept transparently without implying availability;
- cost in time and money;
- opportunity to learn from real audience behaviour;
- privacy and health-claim risks;
- ability to measure a meaningful outcome;
- moderation and support requirements.

Possible channels to evaluate—not a recommendation that all should be used—include direct research outreach, educational content, relevant communities where permitted, founder-led updates, and a clearly labelled concept page. Do not post promotional health claims into communities without understanding their rules and audience needs.

## Recommended sequence
1. Confirm the current goal and real CTA.
2. Conduct research before scaling content distribution.
3. Test a small number of channels with a clear learning question.
4. Track source, message, audience, and outcome consistently.
5. Continue only when evidence and responsible operating capacity support it.

## Constraints
No budget, channel benchmark, conversion rate, or audience reach is supplied in the reviewed evidence. Any numeric forecast must be labelled as an assumption and accompanied by its calculation and uncertainty.
