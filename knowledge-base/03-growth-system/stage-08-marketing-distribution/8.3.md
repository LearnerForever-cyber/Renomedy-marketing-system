# 8.3 — CTA, Funnel and Launch-State Controls

**Status:** Working draft — owner approval pending

## CTA truth gate
The reviewed source register does not establish a functioning waitlist or other verified conversion destination. Do not use “Download now,” “Start using,” “Join the waitlist,” “Get early access,” or similar language until the owner confirms the offer, destination, eligibility, and that the path works end to end.

## Concept-stage funnel (proposed)
1. **Exposure:** a person sees accurate concept-stage content.
2. **Understanding:** they can explain what Renomedy is exploring and what is not available.
3. **Research engagement:** they may voluntarily complete an approved interview or concept-feedback step.
4. **Learning:** feedback is recorded with consent and limitations.
5. **Decision:** evidence informs whether to refine the concept, audience, or next research step.

This is a proposed learning funnel, not a claim that the funnel currently exists or has measured performance.

## Launch-state controls
Before any campaign, verify the canonical site, current product status, CTA destination, audience eligibility, privacy notice, data collection, and support expectations. If any critical element is unknown, remove the acquisition CTA and request owner review.

## Reporting
Report clicks, form starts, completions, qualified research participation, and drop-off only when those events are actually instrumented and the definitions are documented. Do not invent funnel values or infer product demand from impressions alone.
