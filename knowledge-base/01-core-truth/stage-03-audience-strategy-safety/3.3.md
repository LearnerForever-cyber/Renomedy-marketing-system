# 3.3 — Safety, Consent and Responsible Communication

**Status:** Working draft — owner approval pending

## Non-negotiable health boundary
Existing public-source records say Renomedy is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendation and does not replace healthcare professionals. Follow safety/health-claims-and-guardrails.md for exact claim boundaries.

Never imply that Renomedy independently determines a safe dose, interprets uncertain instructions authoritatively, or guarantees medicine use or health outcomes.

## Consent and caregiver assumptions
A family relationship does not automatically grant access to another person's health information or authority to make decisions. Avoid copy that treats parents as passive subjects or assumes adult children should control a parent's routine. Where relevant, emphasize the person's agency and the need for appropriate permission.

Any future data-sharing or permission workflow is unverified until demonstrated and documented. Do not promise privacy controls merely because they would be desirable.

## Responsible research and marketing
- Use respectful, non-stigmatizing language for older adults and caregivers.
- Avoid fear, shame, guilt, or urgency that exploits health concerns.
- Do not solicit identifiable prescription images, medicine lists, or private health details in public channels.
- Do not invent patient stories, testimonials, statistics, or endorsements.
- Separate educational information from product promotion.
- Make the development status clear wherever a person might otherwise expect to use the product.
- Route ambiguous health claims, sensitive-data practices, or regulatory implications to appropriate human review.

## Escalation rule
If a proposed message could reasonably lead a reader to make a medication decision, share sensitive data without understanding the implications, or rely on an unverified feature, pause publication. Record the exact claim, why it is risky, what evidence is missing, and who must review it.
