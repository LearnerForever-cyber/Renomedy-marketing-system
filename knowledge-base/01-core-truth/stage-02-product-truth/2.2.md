# 2.2 — Concept Scope & Intended User Tasks

**Status:** Working draft — owner approval pending

## What the concept is organized around
The public-site snapshot recorded in S1 and the internal project context describe a concept around:
- organizing everyday medicine-routine details;
- keeping relevant family context together;
- supporting coordination between people involved in family care.

**Evidence label:** Publicly stated in the dated S1 snapshot for the public framing; broader capability detail is internal vision.  
**Sources:** [Source Register](../../sources/source-register.md), [Fact Ledger](../../sources/fact-ledger.md), and owner-provided Renomedy Context (S2).

These are descriptions of the product direction, not a list of available features.

## Intended user tasks — hypotheses, not proof of demand
The internal context identifies several user groups and needs. Treat them as candidate use cases to investigate, not validated customer findings.

| Candidate task | Evidence status | Safe interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Keep medicine-routine information organized | Public concept framing; implementation unknown | An intended problem area, not proof a working organizer exists |
| Help family members coordinate around care-related information | Public concept framing; actual sharing and permissions unknown | An intended coordination problem, not proof of family sharing |
| Make prescription information easier to understand | Internal product vision; live capability unverified | Future/intended direction only unless a working, reviewed capability is evidenced |
| Record scheduled dose events | Internal product vision; live capability unverified | Do not claim tracking is available |
| Keep visibility on medicine supply and possible refill timing | Internal product vision; live capability unverified | Do not claim supply prediction is available |
| Support adult children helping parents | Audience named in the public-site snapshot and internal context; demand not validated | A plausible segment to test, not the proven best customer segment |

## Product scope boundaries
Renomedy must not be described as a doctor, pharmacist, diagnostic system, or treatment provider; an authority that changes a prescription or recommends a dose; a guarantee that a medicine was taken, will be available, or will not be missed; or a replacement for prescription labels, pharmacists, clinicians, or other authoritative care instructions.

These boundaries are consistent with the repository's [Health Claims, Trust and Safety Guardrails](../../safety/health-claims-and-guardrails.md).

## Distinguish the user, record, and real-world event
Keep a clear distinction between:
- **Prescription/document information:** what a source document appears to say.
- **Stored medication information:** what a user or system has entered or extracted.
- **Schedule:** an intended or recorded plan.
- **Dose-event record:** a record that someone logged an event.
- **Observed real-world event:** what actually happened, which may not be fully knowable from a digital record alone.
- **Supply estimate:** a calculation based on inputs and assumptions, not a guarantee.

Do not collapse these distinctions into certainty. For example, an unrecorded dose event is not proof that a person did not take the medicine.
