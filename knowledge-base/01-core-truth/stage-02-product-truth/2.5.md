# 2.5 — Clinical, Safety, Privacy & Trust Boundaries

**Status:** Working draft — owner approval pending

## Non-negotiable clinical boundary
The current public-site snapshot says Renomedy is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment recommendation and does not replace healthcare professionals. The same rule is recorded in the repository's safety guardrails.

**Evidence label:** Publicly stated in the S1 snapshot; repeated in the repository safety record.  
**Source:** [Health Claims, Trust and Safety Guardrails](../../safety/health-claims-and-guardrails.md), [Source Register](../../sources/source-register.md).

Renomedy must not be presented as a clinician, pharmacist, diagnostic service, treatment provider, or authority to change prescribed medication use. Medication decisions must remain grounded in the prescription and appropriate professional guidance.

## Claims that are prohibited unless specifically evidenced and approved
Do not claim that Renomedy:
- diagnoses a condition or recommends treatment or dose changes;
- guarantees a user will not miss a dose or make a medication mistake;
- prevents hospitalizations or adverse outcomes;
- perfectly interprets every prescription;
- proves that a medicine was taken when an event is merely recorded;
- guarantees that medicine supply will last until a particular date;
- is clinically validated, certified, regulator-approved, or endorsed by a medical institution;
- uses specific encryption, security controls, certifications, audits, or privacy protections that have not been verified.

A general statement that trust and safety matter is a design principle, not evidence that a particular control has been implemented.

## Record and real-world truth
Keep a clear distinction between what a prescription or label appears to say; what information a user entered; what an automated system extracted or inferred; what someone recorded as an event; and what is independently known to have happened.

An absence of a record is not proof that an event did not occur. An estimate is not a guarantee. An automated interpretation is not a clinical decision.

## Privacy and sensitive information
The reviewed materials do not establish the current implementation of Renomedy's privacy or security controls. Therefore:
- do not make absolute privacy or security promises;
- do not claim encryption, access controls, account deletion, retention rules, audits, or regulatory compliance without current evidence;
- do not ask people to post identifiable prescription images or private health information in public marketing channels;
- use fictional or properly de-identified examples for public demonstrations unless appropriate consent and safeguards are documented;
- route questions about data handling to a verified, owner-approved privacy statement rather than guessing.

## Required review for health-related claims
Any copy that describes prescription interpretation, dose tracking, reminders, refill prediction, clinical outcomes, privacy, or security must be reviewed against current evidence and receive human approval. Automated content checks do not replace human review.

## Source basis and limitation
The repository safety guardrails are the controlling internal policy for marketing outputs. They are not proof that product controls have been implemented.
