## Availability language
Until a newer status is verified and approved:
- Say **“in development”** or **“a concept being developed”** where relevant.
- Do not say users can download, install, subscribe to, or start using the product now.
- Do not promise a launch date.
- Do not imply that website mock-ups are screens from a live application.
- Do not invent a waitlist, sign-up flow, demo, or conversion destination. Confirm that the destination exists and works before referring users to it.

## Category language
**Working category:** family medicine-routine organization and coordination.

Use this as a plain-language description of the intended problem space, not as proof of market category leadership or a verified product capability. “Medication intelligence” appears in internal vision material; use it cautiously because it could imply more clinical or automated capability than has been established.
