**Rule:** Do not infer implementation from product vision, mock-ups, technology exploration, or plans.

## Status vocabulary
- **Live:** verified as available to intended users in the current product, with current evidence.
- **Prototype:** a demonstrable working experiment, not necessarily production-ready or available to users.
- **Planned:** explicitly intended for a future build; not available now.
- **Dropped:** explicitly decided not to pursue; record the decision source/date.
- **Unknown:** current status cannot be established from the available evidence.

## How to update this matrix
Every status change must include:
1. capability name and precise scope;
2. status (Live, Prototype, Planned, Dropped, or Unknown);
3. evidence artifact or source URL;
4. evidence date and person/role confirming it;
5. environment and user availability, if applicable;
6. limitations, known failure modes, and dependencies;
7. whether public wording is approved.

For a Live claim, a plan, design mock-up, screenshot, or internal assertion alone is insufficient. Prefer direct current demonstration or release evidence plus owner confirmation. For health-related functionality, product availability does not itself prove clinical accuracy, safety, or validation.
