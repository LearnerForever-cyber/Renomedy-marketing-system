# 1.1 — Company Identity & Definition

**Status:** Working draft — owner approval pending

## Working definition
Renomedy is being developed as a family medication-intelligence concept intended to help people coordinating care for themselves or relatives organize everyday medicine-routine information and share relevant family context.

This describes the product direction, not a claim that a functioning app or specific feature is available. The current public website says Renomedy is in development, not yet available, and that the screens shown are illustrative concepts.

## Category and framing
A useful working category is **family medication organization and coordination**. “Medication intelligence” may describe the broader internal ambition, but it must not imply that Renomedy makes clinical decisions or independently determines what a person should take.

The concept concerns the practical work families do to keep medicine-related information understandable and coordinated. It does not replace a clinician, pharmacist, prescription label, or authoritative care instruction.

## What Renomedy is trying to be
- A practical aid for organizing medicine-routine details.
- A way to make relevant family context easier to coordinate.
- A trust-first product concept for a sensitive everyday care task.
- A product that helps people review information with qualified professionals, not bypass them.
- A product whose public claims match verified availability and behavior.

## What Renomedy must not present itself as
Unless appropriate evidence and review support a specific statement, do not describe Renomedy as:
- A doctor, pharmacist, diagnostic system, or treatment provider.
- A replacement for prescriptions, clinical instructions, or professional advice.
- A guarantee against medication mistakes, adverse events, hospital visits, or other clinical outcomes.
- A proven, clinically validated, certified, or regulator-approved product.
- A currently available app or a bundle of live features while public status remains “in development / not yet available.”

## Naming and language
- Use Renomedy as the product/company name in normal prose.
- The established public headline is “Care works better, together.”
- Do not invent a company origin story, founder story, acronym, or linguistic meaning.
- Preserve approved brand assets rather than reopening settled visual decisions without a clear reason.
- The established preference is to retain the current landing-page typography; verify actual approved assets before treating detailed font specifications as canonical.

## Audience framing
At company-definition level, Renomedy serves people involved in organizing medicine-related information and coordinating care with family. More specific segments, such as adult children helping parents, remain hypotheses to evaluate, not proof of the best converting audience.

## Definition quality test
A description is suitable only if it explains the coordination problem in ordinary language, avoids unverified feature claims, avoids clinical authority or outcome promises, matches current public status, and remains useful if the feature set changes.
