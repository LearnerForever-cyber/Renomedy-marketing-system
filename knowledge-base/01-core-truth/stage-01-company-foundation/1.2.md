# 1.2 — Purpose, Mission, Vision & Intended Impact

**Status:** Working draft — owner approval pending  
These statements are drafts for alignment, not claims of achieved impact or approved external copy.

## Purpose — why Renomedy should exist
**Draft purpose:** Make the everyday work of coordinating medicine-related information within families clearer, more manageable, and easier to discuss with qualified professionals.

The purpose should stay grounded in a recognizable human task. It should not depend on speculative AI capabilities, fear-based messaging, or promises of clinical outcomes.

## Mission — what Renomedy is working toward
**Draft mission:** Build a trustworthy, family-oriented way to organize everyday medicine-routine details and share relevant context, while keeping people and qualified healthcare professionals in control of health decisions.

## Vision — the future Renomedy hopes to help create
**Draft vision:** A future in which families can coordinate medicine-related information with less confusion and friction, and approach care conversations with better-organized context.

This is an aspiration, not a claim that Renomedy has already reduced confusion, improved adherence, or produced measurable health benefits.

## Intended impact chain
1. Families encounter medicine-related information that can be difficult to coordinate.
2. Better organization and clearer shared context may make information easier to find and review.
3. Easier review may support more productive conversations among family members and qualified professionals.
4. If supported by research, this may contribute to a less confusing coordination experience.

Every step is a hypothesis until evidence supports it. Better organization alone does not establish improved adherence, fewer medication errors, better health, or reduced healthcare utilization.

## What success may mean by maturity level
### Before product availability
- The problem is described clearly and without exaggeration.
- Research distinguishes frequent, painful problems from merely interesting ones.
- Product and marketing claims stay aligned with verified status.
- Trust and safety concerns are surfaced early.

### During early product validation
- Users can complete the intended core task in a prototype or verified build.
- Research shows where users misunderstand, hesitate, or lose trust.
- Repeat use and real-world usefulness are examined, not only positive reactions.
- Safety, privacy, permissions, and accessibility questions are documented.

### After availability, if confirmed
- Usage and retention are measured with clear definitions.
- User-reported usefulness is separated from clinical outcomes.
- Health-related outcome claims require appropriate evidence and review.
- Growth is assessed alongside trust, comprehension, support burden, and harm signals.

These are proposed measurement directions, not assertions that instrumentation exists.

## Strategic non-goals
Do not optimize for growth at the expense of trust, claim accuracy, or user comprehension. Do not increase anxiety to generate engagement, present uncertainty as certainty, or turn sensitive care into fear-driven conversion copy.

## Owner decisions
- Approve, revise, or replace the purpose, mission, and vision.
- Decide whether the first strategic focus is family coordination, personal organization, or a narrower validated use case.
- Define which outcomes Renomedy is willing and able to measure responsibly.
