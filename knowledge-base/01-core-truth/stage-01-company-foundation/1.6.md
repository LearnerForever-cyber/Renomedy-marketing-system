# 1.6 — Decision Rules, Evidence Status & Open Questions

**Status:** Proposed governance rules — owner approval pending

## Evidence status vocabulary
- **Publicly stated:** Explicitly present in a dated public source. This proves what the source says, not necessarily what the product implements.
- **Owner-context:** Supplied in internal project material; useful for direction, but not automatically externally verified.
- **Approved:** Explicitly approved by the owner for a stated purpose and scope. Record date and supporting evidence where relevant.
- **Verified behavior:** Directly checked in the relevant running product or environment, with date and test scope recorded.
- **Hypothesis:** A proposition to investigate.
- **Unconfirmed:** Available evidence is insufficient.
- **Superseded:** Replaced by a newer decision or source; preserve history and link the replacement.

Do not collapse these labels into a generic “fact” status.

## Source hierarchy
For current public product status and marketing claims:
1. Current owner-approved facts supported by relevant verification.
2. Current public Renomedy website for what is communicated to prospective users.
3. Owner-provided internal context for long-term vision and intended capabilities.
4. Prior project conversations for plans and hypotheses.
5. AI inference, which must never be promoted to fact without confirmation.

When sources conflict, flag the conflict and request resolution. The existing source register and fact ledger remain the reference records for provenance and claim status.

## Decision record minimum
For a consequential decision, record a short title/ID, date, accountable human, context, options, decision and rationale, evidence and confidence, risks and trade-offs, reversibility, reconsideration triggers, and related documents or experiments. An agent may draft a record but must not label it owner-approved without explicit approval.

## Default decision rules
1. When evidence is insufficient, preserve uncertainty and choose the lowest-risk useful next step.
2. For current availability or capability claims, require current verification.
3. For decisions affecting health, privacy, consent, or safety, escalate rather than infer.
4. When documents conflict, flag the mismatch and identify the sources.
5. When a decision is superseded, preserve history and link the replacement.
6. If an action depends on a missing fact, ask for confirmation or choose a safe alternative.
7. Do not reopen an established decision merely because an agent has a new preference. Reopen only when new evidence, changed constraints, a clear inconsistency, or an explicit owner request justifies review.

## Open-question workflow
Maintain unresolved questions in the existing strategy/open-questions file or a relevant stage document. Each important question should include why it matters, current status, evidence needed, accountable person, next action, and review date. Close it only when the answer is supported and the relevant source or decision record is updated. Silence is not approval.

## Stage 1 exit criteria
- Company definition is understandable and avoids unverified capability claims.
- Purpose, mission, and vision are explicitly drafts until approved.
- Principles and decision rules are clear enough for agents to apply.
- Business-model assumptions are separated from decisions.
- Known facts, hypotheses, and unknowns are documented.
- Open decisions are visible and linked to evidence records.
