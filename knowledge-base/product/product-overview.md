# Product Overview and Status

## Current public status — highest priority for marketing

**Renomedy is in development and not yet available.** The current public website states that its screens are illustrative concepts, not a live product, and that the concept/design may change as the team learns from families.

Source: `https://getrenomedy.framer.website/` (checked 2026-10-22; see source register).

## What the public website currently describes

The public concept explores:
- Keeping everyday medicine-routine details organized.
- Bringing relevant family context or notes together.
- Helping family members stay informed about routine details.
- Making it easier to understand how family members might contribute to everyday care.
- Supporting coordination alongside guidance from healthcare professionals.

These are descriptions of the concept and intended direction, not confirmation that those capabilities are implemented.

## Broader internal product vision

The owner-provided project context describes a wider long-term ambition around family medication intelligence, including:
- AI-assisted prescription understanding.
- Organizing medication information.
- Recording dose-related events.
- Coordinating family caregiving.
- Estimating potential medicine-supply gaps.

**Status:** Internal vision / intended capability areas. Not verified as shipped, available, clinically validated, or promised on the current public website.

## Technology exploration — not a production stack

The owner-provided project context mentions that ML Kit, Tesseract, MedGemma, Groq and Gemini have been explored. This does not establish that all, or any specific combination, is currently integrated into a production pipeline.

The available context does not reliably confirm the current production frontend, backend, database, authentication, hosting, model configuration or deployment architecture. Do not invent those details.

## Critical distinctions

- A concept screen is not a functioning feature.
- OCR extracts text; it does not verify that prescription information is correct.
- AI interpretation can be uncertain or wrong.
- A scheduled event is not proof a medicine was taken.
- An unrecorded event is not proof a medicine was missed.
- A supply estimate depends on accurate and complete information.
- An internal roadmap is not automatically an approved public claim.

## Marketing restriction

Until the owner confirms a feature is implemented and ready for users, describe it only as part of the broader vision when appropriate, and clearly frame it as planned/exploratory. Do not include it in public campaign copy as an existing capability.
