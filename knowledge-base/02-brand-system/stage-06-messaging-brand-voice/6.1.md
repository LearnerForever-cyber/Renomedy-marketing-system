# 6.1 — Messaging Hierarchy and Claim Boundaries

**Status:** Draft messaging system — owner approval pending

## Current recorded headline
“Care works better, together.” is the public headline recorded in source register S1. The last recorded access date is 2026-10-22; fresh retrieval failed on 2026-10-24. Confirm current wording before a time-sensitive campaign.

## Proposed message hierarchy
1. **Stage truth:** Renomedy is in development and not yet available, according to the latest recorded public-site snapshot.
2. **Human problem space:** everyday medicine-routine information and family context can be difficult to coordinate; treat this as a problem hypothesis until user research supports stronger claims.
3. **Concept direction:** exploring ways to help people and families organize relevant routine details and context.
4. **Trust boundary:** not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendation; does not replace healthcare professionals.
5. **Call to action:** use only a destination that has been checked and approved; otherwise prefer a non-acquisition educational or research invitation approved by the owner.

This hierarchy is proposed for review, not a fully approved brand architecture.

## Claim classes
- **Allowed with source/date:** name, recorded headline, current public concept wording.
- **Concept-only:** planned capabilities, future product direction, proposed user benefits.
- **Hold:** feature availability, launch date, price, waitlist, security controls, clinical validation, accuracy, outcome improvements, traction, or testimonials without evidence.
- **Prohibited:** invented evidence, medical advice claims, guarantees, or statements that could encourage reliance on unverified medication interpretation.
