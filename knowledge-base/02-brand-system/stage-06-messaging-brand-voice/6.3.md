# 6.3 — Copy Patterns, Prohibited Claims and Review

**Status:** Working draft — owner approval pending

## Safer concept-stage patterns
- “Renomedy is exploring ways to help families organize everyday medicine-routine details.”
- “A concept for bringing routine details and family context together.”
- “Designed around family coordination, alongside guidance from healthcare professionals.”

Use only where consistent with the current public status and approved campaign purpose. These patterns are not claims that features are available.

## Do not publish without specific evidence and approval
- “Download now,” “Start using,” or “Sign up” unless availability and destination are real and verified.
- “Reads every prescription accurately,” “never miss a dose,” or guarantees of supply continuity.
- Clinical validation, certification, encryption, audits, endorsements, partnerships, user counts, testimonials, or outcomes that are not evidenced.
- Competitive superlatives such as first, best, or unique without substantiation.
- Quotes or stories attributed to people who have not provided valid permission.

## Review checklist
1. Identify every factual and implied claim.
2. Map each claim to a source and status in the fact ledger.
3. Check current availability and CTA destination.
4. Remove or label future capability claims.
5. Check health, privacy, safety, and consent implications.
6. Ask a human reviewer to approve sensitive or material claims.
7. Save final copy, source links, reviewer, and approval date.

An AI lint pass is not publication clearance.
