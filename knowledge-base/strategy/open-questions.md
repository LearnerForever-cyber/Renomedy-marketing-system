# Open Questions — Owner Approval Required

Do not connect this knowledge base to production campaign generation until the owner has reviewed the most important questions.

## Immediate launch-status questions

- Is the current public website the canonical landing page?
- Should all marketing currently be concept-awareness and customer-validation content rather than product acquisition?
- Is there a waitlist, form or other real conversion destination? If yes, what is the exact URL?
- What is the current product development status beyond what the public website says?

## Feature status

For each capability, mark: `Live`, `Prototype`, `Planned`, `Dropped`, or `Unknown`.
- Routine overview / everyday medicine-routine details.
- Family notes or context.
- Family sharing and permissions.
- Prescription image upload or OCR.
- AI-assisted prescription interpretation.
- Medication schedules.
- Dose-event tracking.
- Reminders or notifications.
- Supply tracking / refill estimates.
- Accounts, authentication and data deletion.

## Audience and research

- Is adult children managing parents' medicines the first segment to target?
- What interviews, observations or user feedback have been collected?
- Which pain points have been confirmed versus inferred?
- Which region and languages are first priority?

## Brand assets

- What are the approved logo files, colors, fonts and visual rules?
- Is “Reno” a mascot/character, and what are its approved use rules?
- Which brand lines are approved as canonical?
- Which visual styles should be avoided?

## Trust and safety

- What privacy and security measures are actually implemented?
- Has any clinical, regulatory or security claim been independently verified?
- Which health-related statements require owner or professional review?

## Growth

- What is the primary current CTA?
- Which channels are active?
- Are there any validated conversion or retention baselines?
- What is the next customer-validation milestone?
