# Brand Identity

## Publicly stated identity

- **Name:** Renomedy. [Publicly stated]
- **Current public headline:** “Care works better, together.” [Publicly stated]
- **Current public status:** In development; not yet available. [Publicly stated]
- **Current public concept:** A shared place for families to keep everyday medicine-routine details organized and stay on the same page. [Publicly stated]
- **Public themes:** Clarity, shared responsibility, everyday routine details, family context, coordination and support alongside healthcare professionals. [Publicly stated]
- **Public boundary:** Not a source of medical advice, diagnosis or treatment recommendations; not a replacement for healthcare professionals. [Publicly stated]

## Working brand principles

These are summaries of the site's language, not claims of a formally approved brand book.

- **Clarity:** Make everyday routine information easier to organize.
- **Togetherness:** Help family members share relevant context.
- **Thoughtful support:** Recognize that care can involve several people.
- **Respectful boundaries:** Support everyday coordination, not medical decision-making.

## Working description

Renomedy is a concept in development exploring how families can organize everyday medicine-routine details and share the context that helps them coordinate care.
