# Weekly workflow

1. **Research (30–45 min):** collect public questions and consented interview notes; remove identifying details.
2. **Prioritize (20 min):** choose one audience problem and 3–5 post concepts.
3. **Draft (60–90 min):** create carousel/Reel/Story drafts from approved briefs.
4. **Review (20–30 min):** check evidence, product truth, privacy, brand and accessibility.
5. **Approve:** a named human approves each post. No auto-publishing in v0.
6. **Publish:** publish manually and log the live URL.
7. **Measure:** record metrics after 48 hours and 7 days.
8. **Learn (20 min):** document one repeat, one change and one unresolved question.
