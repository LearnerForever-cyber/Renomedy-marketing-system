# Launch readiness checklist

## Account readiness
- [ ] Confirm Instagram handle and profile image.
- [ ] Confirm the product stage (exploring, prototype, beta, or live).
- [ ] Confirm the landing-page URL and check all public copy against current product truth.
- [ ] Add bio, highlights and pinned posts only after review.

## Creative readiness
- [ ] Review the 3 carousel slide sequences in order.
- [ ] Review all captions, CTA wording, alt text and hashtags.
- [ ] Check readability and safe margins on a phone.
- [ ] Replace the placeholder mascot mark with approved brand artwork if required.
- [ ] Add native poll stickers to Story backgrounds.
- [ ] Record/edit the three Reel scripts; covers alone are not video assets.

## Safety and approval
- [ ] Human owner verifies health-related wording.
- [ ] Human owner confirms all product-stage claims.
- [ ] No individualized advice, medication changes, clinical promises or invented evidence.
- [ ] No prompts for private health information in public comments.
- [ ] Human owner explicitly approves each post. AI review PASS is not approval.

## Publishing and learning
- [ ] Publish manually.
- [ ] Record published URL and timestamp.
- [ ] Log reach, saves, shares, profile visits, follows and useful non-sensitive feedback after 24–72 hours.
- [ ] Record one learning per post and use it to adjust the next batch.

## Launch decision
The creative batch is prepared. The account is ready to start posting only after the human review gates above are checked and any remaining video edits/profile setup are completed.
