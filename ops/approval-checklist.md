# Pre-publish checklist

## Accuracy and safety
- [ ] Every medical/health claim has a reliable source.
- [ ] Necessary claims have qualified human review.
- [ ] No individualized advice or medication-change instructions.
- [ ] No fear, shame, guarantees or unsupported outcome claims.
- [ ] No invented statistics, testimonials, partnerships or credentials.

## Product and privacy
- [ ] Every product capability mentioned is verified and current.
- [ ] No unverified privacy/security/compliance promises.
- [ ] No personal health details requested in public.
- [ ] Images/screenshots contain no records, labels or private information.

## Content quality
- [ ] One clear takeaway.
- [ ] Accessible language and legible design.
- [ ] Caption and CTA match the actual post.
- [ ] Alt text/accessibility checked.
- [ ] Human owner explicitly approved.
