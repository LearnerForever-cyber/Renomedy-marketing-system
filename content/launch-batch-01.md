# Instagram launch batch 01

This is the first fully prepared organic content batch. The downloadable ZIP created for this project contains the post-ready PNG assets and a copy guide.

## Assets in the launch pack

- 3 five-slide carousels (15 PNG slides)
- 3 static feed post PNGs
- 4 Instagram Story background PNGs (two two-frame sequences)
- 3 Reel cover PNGs
- 11 captions, hashtag suggestions and alt-text descriptions
- 3 short Reel scripts
- Draft profile bio, suggested sequence and a launch checklist

## Draft profile setup

**Name:** Renomedy | Family Medication Clarity

Use the verified landing-page URL only after checking it reflects the current product stage. Pin the appointment checklist, brand-positioning post, and unclear-instructions post.

## Non-negotiable review gates

- Confirm the product stage and ensure no copy implies unverified features are live.
- Human-review every health-related statement and every caption before posting.
- Do not claim clinical outcomes, certifications, privacy guarantees, or features that have not been confirmed.
- Do not ask followers to post medicine names, diagnoses, prescriptions, or other private health details.
- Story assets are backgrounds only: add native Instagram poll stickers in the app.
- Reel covers and scripts are ready, but the video itself must still be recorded/edited.
- Graphics use a provisional palette sampled from the current concept board and a placeholder icon; replace with the approved logo/mascot if required before public launch.
- Publish manually. No automatic Instagram publishing is configured.
