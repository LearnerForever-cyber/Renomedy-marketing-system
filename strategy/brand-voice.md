# Brand voice

## Principles
Clear, calm, practical, respectful, warm and family-aware. Avoid alarmism, guilt, hospital-brochure stiffness and miracle-AI positioning.

## Writing rules
- One core idea per post.
- Use plain language and define unavoidable medical terms.
- Prefer “help organize information” over “ensure safety” or “prevent errors.”
- Never imply a post replaces a doctor or pharmacist.
- Avoid fear hooks, exaggerated urgency, cure claims and “you've been doing it wrong” framing.
- Ask for feedback about workflows, not diagnoses, medicines or prescriptions in public comments.

## Product truth
Do not describe a feature as available unless the product team confirms it. Use “we're exploring” or “we're building” only when accurate. Never invent customer numbers, outcomes, testimonials, partnerships, certifications or privacy guarantees.
