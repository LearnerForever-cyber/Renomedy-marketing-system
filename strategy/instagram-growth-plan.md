# Instagram organic growth plan — v0.1

## Goal
Learn which helpful, trustworthy topics resonate with people coordinating medication routines for themselves or family members. Early success is learning and qualified engagement, not follower count alone.

## Audience hypotheses to validate
1. Family coordinators helping a parent or relative keep track of medicines.
2. Busy adults managing routines across family members.
3. People who leave appointments with unclear instructions and want to organize questions.
4. Caregivers who need a clearer way to coordinate responsibilities and refill planning.

These are hypotheses, not proven segments. Validate through interviews, comments, polls and landing-page behavior.

## Content pillars
- **Clarity:** organizing information and preparing questions for a clinician or pharmacist.
- **Continuity:** routine and refill-planning habits without changing prescribed instructions.
- **Coordination:** respectful ways families can share responsibilities.
- **Trust:** what Renomedy is, what it is not, and transparent product learning.
- **Build in public:** only share product status or learnings that the team has verified.

## Format experiments
- Carousels: saveable checklists and practical explainers.
- Reels: one small problem, one useful takeaway, one gentle CTA.
- Stories: polls, general question prompts and behind-the-scenes learning.
- Founder posts: explain the problem being explored and invite workflow feedback.

## CTA ladder
1. Save this checklist / share with a family coordinator.
2. Ask a general question about coordination challenges.
3. Invite workflow feedback privately; never ask people to post personal health details publicly.
4. Use only a verified waitlist/demo link and clearly explain what it offers.

## 30-day experiment
- Week 1: 2 carousels + 1 Story poll; test clarity vs coordination.
- Week 2: 2 Reels + 1 carousel; test practical tips vs caregiver empathy.
- Week 3: 2 carousels + 2 Story prompts; test checklist vs clarification formats.
- Week 4: repeat the strongest topic in another format; share verified learnings.

If 3 posts/week is not sustainable, start with 2 strong posts and Stories.

## Metrics
Primary: saves/reach, shares/reach, meaningful comments/DMs, profile visits, link clicks and qualified waitlist/demo interest if available.
Secondary: reach, Reel retention where available, and follows attributed to posts.
Guardrails: negative feedback, confusion, unsafe interpretations and correction requests.

Compare similar formats over multiple posts. Do not infer causality from a small sample.

## Weekly learning questions
- What problem language do people use themselves?
- Which posts are saved or shared because they are useful?
- Which topics need qualified review?
- Do people understand Renomedy's current scope?
- Which CTA generates meaningful conversations without collecting sensitive information?
